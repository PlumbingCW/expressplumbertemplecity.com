Disposal leaking or sink backed up: act now

A disposal that leaks under the sink is dripping into the cabinet and onto the floor. One that is jammed and humming has a stuck impeller, and one that is silent has lost power or tripped its reset. A sink full of water with the disposal running is a downstream clog.

Switch the disposal off at the wall switch, never put a hand into it and put a bucket under the leak. If water is escaping fast, close the shutoffs under the sink. We take these calls at night and on weekends, and same-day visits are available when we have an opening.

Under the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), a disposal needs a trap and a proper drain connection, and the dishwasher drain needs a high loop or air gap.

Repair or replace

A jam or a tripped reset is a cheap fix. A leak from the housing is not repairable, because the unit is sealed. A disposal that has been running for many years, jams often or has rusted through is better replaced.

When we replace one, we match the size of the motor to your kitchen, reconnect the drain and dishwasher line and test for leaks. A cabinet that has had a leak may also have damaged supply valves, which are repaired as described on our faucet and valve repair page.

What belongs in a disposal and what does not

Disposals handle soft food scraps. They struggle with fibrous vegetable peels, bones, coffee grounds in large amounts, rice and pasta that swell, and grease that cools in the pipe.

Run cold water while the disposal is on and for a few seconds after. If the sink drains slowly after repairs, the next step is clearing the line, which our drain cleaning work covers.

Kitchens in older Temple City homes

Houses from the 1950s make up 30.6 percent of Temple City's housing, and their kitchen drains are often narrow and old. A disposal sends more solids down a line that may already have scale and grease on its walls, so a house that has a disposal installed late in its life can clog sooner than a newer one.

Water hardness matters here too. Golden State Water's South San Gabriel system averaged about 240 mg/L in 2025, which leaves mineral scale that can build on the seals and the grinding surfaces.

If your kitchen is part of a remodel, ask about the sink's drain connection and the dishwasher air gap at the same visit. It is cheaper to correct the plumbing while the cabinet is open.
