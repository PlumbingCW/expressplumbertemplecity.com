Burst pipes, backed-up drains and no hot water in El Monte

Most emergency calls from El Monte start with one of three things. Water is coming from somewhere it should not, a drain has stopped and sewage or gray water is rising, or the hot water is gone. Each one is a same-day problem when we have an opening, and our dispatch line is staffed at night and on weekends.

Start with the shutoffs. For a leak, close the main valve and drain the lines at a low faucet. For a backup, stop using water and leave toilets alone. For no hot water, check the breaker or pilot, and call us if it will not come back. Our burst pipe repair and drain cleaning pages explain what we do next.

Owners, renters and who calls the plumber

El Monte has a large rental market. Of the 16,851 single-family detached homes in the city's housing element, 9,613 are owner-occupied and 7,238 are rented, and that does not count apartments and duplexes. A plumbing emergency in a rental raises a practical question about who should call.

If you rent, tell your landlord or property manager right away and ask whether they have a plumber. If you own the property or manage it for an owner, call us directly. We give owners and managers a clear description of what failed and what we fixed, which is useful for insurance and for the lease.

For rental buildings with shared drains, a backup in one unit often traces to a line shared by several. Our camera shows where the blockage is so the right party handles it.

El Monte water supply and what it does to pipes

The City of El Monte Water Department supplies the city from groundwater in the Main San Gabriel Groundwater Basin, with production wells in El Monte and Rosemead. Its 2025 report lists total hardness from 190 to 260 mg/L, an average of 235 mg/L, and the water is disinfected with chlorine before delivery.

That puts El Monte's water in the harder range, about 14 grains per gallon on average. Scale from water of that kind collects in tank water heaters and narrows fixtures and aerators, and it is one reason that older units in the city fail sooner than their warranty implies. The effects on tanks are described on our water heater repair page.

Public sewer mains are maintained by a public agency, and which one depends on the street. If you are unsure whether a backup is in the main or in your own lateral, call us and we will camera the line and tell you where the problem is.
