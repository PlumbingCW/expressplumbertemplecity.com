We handle the services listed on our plumbing services page. If a job is outside what we do, we say so. We do not promise an arrival time, we do not claim awards we have not earned and we do not publish reviews that are not real.

We do not ask you to fill out forms. You call, and a person answers. That keeps the process fast during an emergency and keeps your details out of an inbox that nobody watches overnight.

How to read a plumbing quote

A good quote names the work, the parts and the total in plain words. Before you say yes to any plumber, ask these questions and expect clear answers.

  • What exactly is included, and what would make the price change?
  • Are parts and labor both in the number you gave me?
  • Does the job need a permit, and who pulls it?
  • What warranty comes with the work, and is it in writing?
  • What will you do about cleanup and any damage to the wall or floor?

We state the price before work starts and we explain the reasons behind it. If the plumber finds something different once the wall is open, we stop, tell you and let you decide.

Why the guides on this site mention Temple City so often

The pages here are written for Temple City homes, not for plumbing in general. According to the city's housing element, 57.7 percent of the homes are more than 60 years old, and most are single-family houses. That shapes what goes wrong: worn supply lines, roots in old drain laterals and water heaters with scale from the local water.

We use those facts to explain why a problem happens and what to ask about it. We do not use them to scare you into work you do not need. If a repair is enough, we say so. If a bigger job is the honest answer, we explain why and let you take your time. Read our blog for examples of how we explain things.

When to call someone other than us first

Some situations come before a plumber. If you smell gas, leave the house at once and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. If water is touching outlets, a panel or a light fixture, turn off power to that area only if you can reach the switch without stepping in water, and otherwise call 911.

If sewage is overflowing from a manhole in the street, that is a public main problem and the county sewer district runs a 24-hour line for it. For anything inside your property line, from a burst supply pipe to a backed-up lateral, call us and we will take it from there.

After the emergency is under control, keep photos and notes on what happened, what you shut off and when. They help with an insurance claim and give the plumber a clear picture of the problem.

Check any plumber's license

Whoever you hire, check that the license is active. The Contractors State License Board offers a License Check on its website at cslb.ca.gov, where you can search by license number or name. It takes a minute and protects you.

California also asks that water heaters be braced against earthquakes, and local building rules apply to many plumbing jobs. Ask the Temple City building department about permits for your project.
