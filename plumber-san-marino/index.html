A city of houses, built early and built out

According to San Marino's own housing element, 98.8 percent of its housing is single-family homes, compared with 54.4 percent across the Southern California region. The city was incorporated in 1913 and was almost completely built out by the 1970s, and the housing element notes that the highest share of homes were built before 1939.

For plumbing, that means there are few new buildings and a great many houses with their original or partly replaced systems. Our emergency dispatch, available around the clock, is built for the old house with a leak in a hidden place.

Large homes, larger consequences

A burst pipe in a two-story house can do damage on both floors before anyone notices. Bigger houses have more bathrooms, longer supply runs, more water heaters and more places for something to fail. In a house of this size, shutting off water fast and knowing where the shutoffs are matters more than anything else.

We help with that over the phone. If the main will not turn or is hidden behind landscaping, dispatch can talk you through the next-best valve, and our burst pipe repair page explains how we stop the water and repair the line.

San Marino water and what it means for old systems

California American Water's San Marino system supplies the city, and its 2023 report listed an average hardness of 162 mg/L, about 9.5 grains per gallon. That is moderately hard, and it still leaves scale on heating elements and in older galvanized pipe.

Houses built in the early twentieth century may have galvanized pipe that has been partly replaced. When we find a mix of materials, we explain where each section starts, and a long-term plan can include repiping the original runs. Water heaters in large houses may be replaced with larger or multiple units, and our water heater installation page explains the planning.

The city's housing element reports that substandard conditions are rarely reported in San Marino, which is a good sign for the building stock. Age still matters, and systems from different decades are often all working in the same house.
