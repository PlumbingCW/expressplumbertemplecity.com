When a faucet or valve leak is an emergency

A slow drip from a spout is not urgent. A broken supply line, a cracked angle stop or a shower valve that will not close is. Water under a sink or behind a tub wall can run for hours before anyone notices, and it soaks cabinets and drywall quickly.

If the shutoff under the sink will not turn, do not force it. Close the main valve at the house and call us. We take these calls at night, on weekends and on holidays, and a same-day visit is available when we have an opening.

California sets maximum flow rates for new faucets under CALGreen and state appliance standards, so a replacement aerator or faucet should match the rated flow of the original.

Shower and tub valves

Shower valves hide inside the wall. A handle that drips after being turned off usually has a worn cartridge, and swapping it takes a short visit. If the valve will not close at all, or water appears at a ceiling below, the valve body may be damaged and an opening in the wall is needed.

A shower or tub that drains slowly is a drain question, not a valve question, and is handled on our drain cleaning page.

Shutoff valves and why old ones fail

Under-sink shutoffs are the valves most people forget until they need them. Many are old multi-turn valves with rubber washers that harden. When someone finally turns one, it breaks, or it will not seal after the repair.

We replace tired valves with quarter-turn ball valves, which close fast and are easier to use in an emergency. If every shutoff in the house is original, replacing them in one visit also makes later repairs easier. For a faucet or fixture that also has a ruined supply pipe, see burst pipe repair.

What hard water does to faucets in Temple City

Golden State Water's South Arcadia system reported an average hardness of 160 mg/L in 2025, and its South San Gabriel system averaged about 240 mg/L. In water of that kind, mineral scale clogs aerators, roughens cartridge seats and makes handles stiff.

That is why a faucet that is a few years old can drip when it should not. Cleaning an aerator is a quick maintenance task, and replacing a cartridge restores the seal. A water filtration or softening system lowers the scale at the source, and the choices are covered under water softener and filtration.
