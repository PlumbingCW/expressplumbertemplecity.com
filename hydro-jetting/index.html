When a backed-up line needs jetting right now

A main line that is full of sewage and will not clear is an emergency, and the first step is usually a cable to open it. Jetting comes in when the blockage is made of something a cable cannot remove, such as thick grease, packed roots or heavy scale, or when the line has backed up repeatedly.

We take these calls after hours and on weekends. While you wait, stop all water use, keep toilets unflushed and lift belongings off floors near drains. If sewage has reached carpet or cabinets, photograph it for your insurer.

What hydro jetting is, in plain terms

A jetting machine pumps water at high pressure through a hose with a nozzle that fires jets forward and backward. The forward jets break up a blockage and the rear jets pull the hose through the pipe and scour the wall clean, pushing debris toward the cleanout.

A cable cuts a hole through a clog. Jetting washes the whole pipe wall, which is why it stops the clog from re-forming in the same spot. Before jetting, we run a camera to check the pipe. Jetting a pipe that is already cracked or collapsed can make it worse, so the camera comes first.

Jetting is not suitable for every pipe. Pipe condition decides the pressure, and standard practice is to look at the line with a camera first.

Symptoms that point to a line that needs more than a cable

  • ✓The main line has been cleared more than once this year and has backed up again.
  • ✓Water drains slowly in every fixture at once.
  • ✓Kitchen drains slow down every few weeks despite regular cleaning.
  • ✓A camera shows grease coating the pipe wall or roots hanging in the line.

If this reads like your house, ask for a camera look and a jetting quote. It also pairs well with the drain cleaning work we do on branch lines.

Where jetting earns its keep in Temple City

Las Tunas Drive is Temple City's main commercial corridor, and restaurant kitchens and apartment buildings put grease into lines faster than a single home does. For those properties, scheduled jetting is routine maintenance, and our commercial plumbing page covers the after-hours side.

For homes, the clogs we jet most often are in lateral lines where the pipe is rough on the inside. Housing in the city skews old, with 88.1 percent single-family homes and more than half of all homes over 60 years old, and rough, aged pipe holds scale and grease that a cable leaves behind.

Los Angeles County's Consolidated Sewer Maintenance District looks after the public mains, so jetting by us is for your lateral and your branch lines only.
