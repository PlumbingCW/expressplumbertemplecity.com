Main line backup: what to do while you wait

The sewer line runs from where all your drains meet to the public main in the street. When it blocks, the water rises in the lowest fixture, which is usually the shower or tub nearest the ground. That is a same-day problem and we treat it as one.

What cleaning a sewer line involves

We open the cleanout, feed a cable machine toward the main and cut through the blockage. Cutting heads shred roots and grease, and a retrieving head pulls out debris. Once the water drains, we run clean water to confirm flow and then pass a camera through to check the pipe.

Cleaning is not the same as repair. It clears a blockage but cannot fix a crack or a sag. If the camera shows damage, we show you the footage and explain what it means, and that conversation leads to sewer line repair when it is needed.

The county sewer maintenance district handles the public main, while the lateral from the house to the main is the property owner's responsibility, according to the district. A camera look shows which side the blockage is on.

Roots, grease, wipes and bellies

Four things cause most main-line blockages. Tree roots enter at joints and cracks, then grow into dense mats. Grease coats the wall and narrows the line. Wipes sold as flushable do not break down like paper and tangle in the pipe. A belly is a sag in the pipe where solids settle and build up.

Cleaning treats the first three directly. A belly needs a repair because the water will keep collecting in the low spot. The camera tells us which one we are dealing with, and we do not guess.

Sewer lines under Temple City streets and yards

The Los Angeles County Consolidated Sewer Maintenance District maintains the public sewer mains that serve Temple City, and it takes reports of overflows on a 24-hour line at (800) 675-4357. Your lateral, from the house to the main, is yours.

If a mature tree stands over your lateral, roots will find clay joints in search of moisture, and a lateral laid in the 1950s may have dozens of them. Since 30.6 percent of the homes in the city date from that decade, a camera look is a sensible habit for any older house.

A cleanout in the front yard or by the house makes the job faster. If your house has none, we can install one, which saves time and money on every future visit.
