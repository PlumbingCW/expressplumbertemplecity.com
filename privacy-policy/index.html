Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information we handle when you call us, email us or visit this website.

Call (626) 898-6169
24/7 Emergency Line Open

Information we receive

When you call (626) 898-6169, we receive your phone number and whatever you tell us, such as your address and the problem you are having. If you email us, we receive your email address and the message. We use this information to answer you, to dispatch a plumber and to run the service.

Call recording

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

This website

This website does not have forms. It does not ask you to create an account. Our web host and standard analytics tools may record technical information such as your browser type and the pages you visit. We use it to keep the site working.

How we share information

We share your address and the details of the problem with the plumber we dispatch so they can help you. We do not use your information for anything unrelated to your service request.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information we hold about you by emailing contact@expressplumbertemplecity.com or calling (626) 898-6169. If you would rather not be recorded, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version is always on this page.
