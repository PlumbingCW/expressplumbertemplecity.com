Where we work from Temple City

Our office is at 9934 E Las Tunas Drive in Temple City, ZIP 91780. Las Tunas Drive is the city's main commercial corridor, and Rosemead Boulevard (State Route 19) runs along its west side. Who handles the pipes outside your property line

Knowing who owns which pipe saves time during an emergency. In Temple City, the public sewer mains belong to the Los Angeles County Consolidated Sewer Maintenance District. If sewage is overflowing from a manhole in the street, the district runs a 24-hour line at (800) 675-4357. The lateral that runs from your house to the main is yours.

Water works the same way in reverse. Your water utility owns the main in the street and the meter, and you own the service line from the meter to the house. Which utility that is depends on your address, because Temple City is served by more than one water system. Our water service line repair page explains how to tell which side of the meter a leak is on.

What we see in each nearby city

Each town page goes deeper, but the short version helps you choose where to start. Arcadia and San Marino are dominated by older single-family houses, so supply lines and drains are often the original ones. El Monte and Rosemead have more rentals and multi-unit buildings, where shared lines and landlord approvals shape how a repair goes. San Gabriel sits between the two, with a mix of old stock and newer infill.

Whatever your address, the first steps are the same. Find the main shutoff, stop using water at the problem fixture and call (626) 898-6169. We take it from there and tell you what we can do the same day.

Help the plumber find you fast

A few small details make arrival quicker in any of these cities. Turn on a porch light at night and make sure the house number is visible from the street. Tell us about a gate code or a locked side yard when you call. Put pets in another room, and leave parking space for a work vehicle close to the house.

In apartment buildings and condominium complexes, give us the unit number and the name of the manager, if you have one. Shared drain stacks and shut-off valves sit in common areas, and the plumber may need someone with a key.

How we decide who to send where

Dispatch goes by address and by the nature of the call. A burst pipe or a sewer backup is treated as same-day when we have an opening, and a planned install is scheduled with you. If your city is not listed above, call anyway. We will tell you honestly whether we can reach you the same day.
