When a water service line leak is urgent

The service line is the pipe that runs underground from the utility's meter to your house. It is the homeowner's responsibility past the meter. When it breaks, water surfaces in the yard or the street, the meter races, and pressure in the house falls.

If you see water bubbling up on the lawn or driveway, close the main valve at the house first. If the leak is on the meter side, call the water utility. If it is between the meter and the house, call us at any hour. We take these calls at night, on weekends and on holidays, and a same-day visit is available when we have an opening.

Signs the underground line is leaking

  • ✓A wet, sunken or unusually green patch in a yard or planter when it has not rained.
  • ✓Water pressure that has dropped across the house.
  • ✓A meter that moves with the house main closed.
  • ✓A water bill that rose with no change in use.
  • ✓The sound of running water near the meter or the front of the house.

These also overlap with irrigation leaks and some interior leaks, so we confirm with a pressure test and a line locate. Our leak detection page describes that survey.

Work on a service line is usually permit work under the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and the water utility owns the meter, so repairs on its side of the line are the utility's.

Repair, replace or reroute the line

A short break in a good pipe can be spot-repaired. A line with several leaks, a corroded steel pipe or a plastic line with a crushed section is usually better replaced from the meter to the house.

Replacement can be done by digging a trench or by pulling a new pipe through the old path in some cases. We discuss which is possible after locating the line and checking what runs above it, such as a driveway, a patio or a tree. A new line also lets us install a shutoff valve and pressure regulator in the right places.

Where the utility's job ends in Temple City

Temple City is served by more than one water system, so the first call depends on your street. Golden State Water Company lists Temple City in its San Gabriel customer service area, and the Cal Am San Marino system reaches some neighboring addresses. The meter marks the boundary, and the utility handles everything on its side of it.

On your side, the pipe from meter to house is yours. In a city where 88.1 percent of housing units are single-family homes, that line is usually a private run across a front yard, past a lawn or a driveway, and sometimes under a mature tree. Roots and soil movement are common causes of breaks.

If the house has low pressure, a failed pressure regulator or a partly closed valve can mimic a service line problem, and we check those first because they are quick. Burst or split interior lines are covered on our burst pipe repair page.
