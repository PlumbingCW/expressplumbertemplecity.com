What to have ready before the plumber arrives

A few minutes of preparation saves time on site. Know where your main water shutoff is and clear a path to it. Move items away from the fixture or wall that is leaking. Take photos of any water damage before you start cleaning, since your insurer may ask for them.

If the problem is in a rental, tell your landlord or property manager as well. Renters often cannot approve a repair that involves the building's lines, so it helps to have that conversation started while the plumber is on the way.

How to choose between a repair and a bigger job

A leak that has happened once is usually a repair. A leak that keeps coming back is a signal about the pipe. In Temple City, 57.7 percent of homes are more than 60 years old, so recurring problems often point to aging supply lines or drains. When that is the case, we say so and explain the options, from a spot fix to repiping or sewer line repair.
