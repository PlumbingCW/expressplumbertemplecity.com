Burst pipe: the first five minutes

Speed matters more than anything else on a burst pipe. The main shutoff is usually at the street meter box or where the supply enters the house. Turn it clockwise until it stops, then open a faucet at the lowest point to drain what remains.

Why pipes burst in a mild climate like Temple City

Freezing is rare in Temple City, so the usual cause is not a hard frost. In houses here, bursts and cracks come from corrosion, pressure and age. Galvanized steel rusts from the inside until a wall gives way. Copper develops pinholes. A joint made decades ago finally lets go.

Pressure plays a part too. A failed or missing pressure regulator pushes more stress into every fitting, so we check the static pressure with a gauge as part of the repair visit.

Fast repairs are one part of the answer. If a burst was caused by pipe condition, another one will follow, which is why we also look at how many other weak points the line has.

Safety comes first. If water has reached outlets, lights or the panel, stay out of the water and switch off power only if you can reach the switch while dry. This is what to do before we arrive when electricity is involved.

How we repair a burst pipe

We shut off the water, locate the break, cut out the damaged section and replace it with new pipe and fittings. Where the pipe is copper, we use soldered or compression fittings as the code allows, and where it is PEX we use approved crimp or expansion connections.

Then we pressure-test the repair, restore water slowly and check nearby joints. We also tell you what we saw in the rest of the line. When the pipe is badly corroded, a spot fix can be a short-term answer and repiping the affected run is the better long-term one.

What we see in Temple City homes

The city's housing element shows that 57.7 percent of Temple City homes are over 60 years old, and the 1950s are the single largest decade at 30.6 percent. A supply line installed then has already had decades of service, and in some houses it has been patched more than once.

Hard water speeds that wear. Golden State Water's South San Gabriel system reported an average hardness of 240 mg/L in 2025. Scale builds inside pipes and corrosion pits the metal under it.

A leak you have not found may be the pipe about to burst, so a leak that has been dripping behind a wall is worth tracking down with leak detection before it fails. If the leak is between the meter and your house, see our water service line repair page.

Water damage and your insurer

A burst pipe is usually a covered event under standard homeowner policies for the damage it causes, though the pipe itself is not. Photograph everything before cleanup, keep receipts and report quickly.

We can give you a written description of what failed and what we repaired, which helps with the claim. Ask your insurer about drying and mitigation help for anything beyond the plumbing.
