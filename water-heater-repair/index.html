When a water heater problem is an emergency

Cold showers are inconvenient. Water on the floor is urgent. If the tank is leaking from the bottom, the leak is almost never fixable and the tank will keep emptying until someone closes the supply. If the relief valve is gushing, the heater is overheating or over-pressured and should be shut down at once.

Here is what helps in the first few minutes, and you can do it while dispatch is on the phone with you.

1Close the cold supplyTurn the valve on the pipe above the tank clockwise until it stops.
2Cut the fuelSet a gas heater's control to off, or flip the electric heater's breaker.
3Drain to a safe spotAttach a hose to the drain valve only if the water is cool and a floor drain is near.
4Call the 24/7 lineCall (626) 898-6169. We can often tell on the phone whether it is a repair or a replacement.

If you smell gas near the heater, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 first, then call us.

No hot water, lukewarm water and what each one means

The symptom narrows the cause fast. No hot water at all on an electric heater often means a tripped breaker, a failed upper element or a bad thermostat. On a gas heater it is more often a pilot that will not stay lit, a failed thermocouple or a gas valve that has stopped opening.

Water that runs hot and then fades to lukewarm in a few minutes points to a failed lower element or a dip tube that has broken down. Water that is hot but runs out quickly can mean sediment has taken up room in the bottom of the tank.

Rumbling or popping noises come from scale and sediment trapped under the heating surface. That is a maintenance signal in Temple City, where the water is on the harder side, and we cover the numbers below.

California law requires a water heater to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquake movement (California Health and Safety Code Section 19211), and the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), calls for a temperature and pressure relief valve with a drain line. Both are worth checking after any water heater repair.

What to do before we arrive: shut the cold water valve above the tank, turn the gas control to off or switch off the breaker, and keep clear of the relief valve drain line, because it can discharge scalding water.

Repair or replace, an honest comparison

A tank water heater is not worth repairing in every case. A new thermostat, element or thermocouple on a heater under about ten years old is a good use of money. A leaking tank, a heater that has needed two repairs in a year, or a unit with heavy corrosion at the fittings is better replaced.

If the numbers favor a new unit, our water heater replacement page explains sizing and what a same-day swap involves, and the tankless water heater page covers the other option.

What hard water does to a water heater in Temple City

Temple City is served by more than one water system. Golden State Water's South San Gabriel system reported 2025 hardness of 189 to 310 mg/L, an average of about 240 mg/L or 14 grains per gallon. Its South Arcadia system averaged 160 mg/L, about 9.4 grains.

Those are utility averages, not a reading from your tap, but the practical effect is the same. Scale forms on heating elements and settles on the bottom of the tank, which lowers efficiency and shortens the life of the heater. Flushing the tank once a year and checking the anode rod are cheap steps that matter more on the harder side of town.

California also requires water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquakes under Health and Safety Code 19211, so any heater we repair or reconnect should have its straps checked at the same visit.

Gas and electric, what is different on the repair

Gas heaters add combustion air, venting and a gas valve to the list of things that can fail, so the plumber checks the draft and the flue as well as the burner. Electric heaters have fewer moving parts, and most faults come down to an element or a thermostat.

Hybrid and heat pump units are less common in older Temple City homes. If you have one, tell us the model when you call so the right parts are on the truck.
