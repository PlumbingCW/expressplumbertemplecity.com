Arcadia's water: wells, two utilities and a wide hardness range

The City of Arcadia gets its water from groundwater, with wells in the Main San Gabriel Basin and the Raymond Basin. Its 2024 water quality report lists total hardness from 23 to 340 mg/L with an average of 210 mg/L, about 12 grains per gallon. Golden State Water's South Arcadia system also serves part of the city and averaged 160 mg/L in 2025, about 9.4 grains.

Hardness that varies this much by well and by street is a practical problem. On the harder end, scale builds on heating elements and tank bottoms, so a water heater in one Arcadia home can fail years before another's a few blocks away. We see the effects most on water heater repair calls, where flushing a tank and replacing an anode rod are the cheap steps that buy time.

Check your bill to see which utility serves you, then read that utility's annual water quality report for its figures. Our water softener and filtration page explains what to do with the numbers.
