Where Live Oak Park is and how we use the name

Live Oak Park is a name used for a residential part of Temple City, and it comes from the city's Live Oak Park, with its community center listed on Bogue Street. Real estate data sites such as NeighborhoodScout also list a Live Oak Park neighborhood in ZIP 91780, defined by census tract.

There is no official map of a Live Oak Park neighborhood published by the city that we could find. We therefore do not draw exact boundaries here. If your address is near the park, this page is meant for you, and if you are unsure, give us the address when you call and we will confirm we serve it.

91780is the only ZIP code for Temple City, including the area around Live Oak Park.
1923is the year Walter P. Temple founded the original townsite, whose south edge was Live Oak Avenue, according to Wikipedia.

Home plumbing calls around a neighborhood park

This is general information for houses and townhomes in a settled residential area like this one, not a claim about any specific street.

Slow drains in kitchens and bathsBuildup in a branch line slows one fixture, while a main line problem slows several at once. Drain cleaning handles both, and we check which one you have first.
Sewer laterals and mature treesRoots can enter a sewer lateral through joints and cracks, and trees are common in residential areas. If several drains are slow or a toilet gurgles, a sewer inspection shows what is in the line.
Water heater leaks and failuresTanks wear out. Pooling water near the base or no hot water means a look at water heater repair, and a tank that has leaked usually needs replacing.
Leaks you cannot seeA bill that rises with no obvious cause, or a damp patch on a slab floor, calls for leak detection before any repair is quoted.
Dripping faucets and fixturesA small drip wastes water daily. Faucet repair is often a short visit.

Owners, renters and shared walls

NeighborhoodScout's summary describes both owners and renters in the area. If you rent, tell your landlord about a leak or a backup as soon as you notice it, since repair responsibility depends on your lease and local rules. If you own, call us directly.

Townhomes and attached homes can share walls and sometimes drain lines. When a backup affects more than one unit, a camera inspection helps show where the blockage is and who needs to be involved.
